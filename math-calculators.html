Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
What order of operations does the scientific calculator use?

Standard precedence: functions (sin, cos, √, log…) and parentheses first, then powers (^, right-associative — 2^3^2 is 512), then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction. The percent sign is postfix: 50% evaluates to 0.5, so 200 + 15% means 200.15; for "200 plus 15 percent of 200" type 200 + 200 × 15%.

Degrees or radians?

The calculator defaults to degrees (DEG), which matches school math and most everyday use. Switch to RAD for calculus and physics — sin(30) is 0.5 in degrees but about −0.988 in radians. Inverse functions (asin, acos, atan) return their answers in the same mode.

Are fraction results reduced automatically?

Yes. Every fraction result is reduced to lowest terms with the greatest common divisor, and improper fractions are also shown as mixed numbers and decimals. Signs are normalized so the denominator is always positive.

Is the data I enter saved anywhere?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. All calculations run inside your browser tab and disappear the moment you close or refresh the page. Your numbers are never transmitted, stored or logged — and the scientific parser evaluates expressions locally without ever using eval().
