Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
Is lorem ipsum real Latin?

Sort of. It's scrambled and truncated Latin from Cicero's "De finibus bonorum et malorum" (45 BC), passed through sixteenth-century type specimen books. It reads like Latin but means almost nothing — which is exactly why it works as filler: the eye sees rhythm and word shapes, not content.

Are the random words real words?

Yes — they're drawn from a built-in bank of common English words, and the min/max length dials filter that pool before every draw. Random letters, on the other hand, are pure uniform chance: one character at a time, with no linguistic structure behind them.

Can I use the generated text freely?

Yes. The output is produced entirely in your browser and is yours to use for anything — mockups, drafts, test data, print layouts, datasets. No attribution is needed, and because nothing is logged, there's no paper trail either.

Is anything saved or uploaded?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. Every run is computed inside your browser tab and disappears the moment you close or refresh the page. There is nothing to leak, because nothing is ever collected.

Can I use these generators offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, every generator keeps working offline — the word bank, the lorem corpus and all the logic run locally in your browser.
