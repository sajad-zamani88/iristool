Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
Do the rounded corners stay transparent in the export?

Yes. The PNG export keeps the corners fully transparent, so you can drop the result straight onto any background. The matching CSS snippet is copied alongside for web use.

How does the watermark preview render text?

The watermark is drawn on a canvas over your image with the opacity, size and position you choose — nine anchor positions or a tiled diagonal mode. A soft shadow keeps light text readable on light photos. The preview and the export are identical.

Does the drawing tool save my sketch?

Only until you leave. The sketch lives on a canvas in your browser tab; use the Download button to keep it as a PNG. Switching to another tool or closing the tab clears it, and nothing is uploaded or stored.

Does my image ever leave my device?

No. Upload, rendering and export all happen on a canvas inside your browser tab. The page has no backend, so there is nowhere for the image to go — not even to a CDN.
