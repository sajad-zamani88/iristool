Decimal to Fraction

Turns any terminating decimal into an exact fraction in lowest terms. The input is read as text, so there is no floating-point rounding anywhere: 0.75 becomes 75/100 and is reduced by the greatest common divisor to 3/4. Improper fractions also come out as mixed numbers.

Example

0.75 · and · 1.2…

↓

3/4 · 6/5 = 1 1/5 — reduced automatically.

02 / 08

Fraction to Decimal

Divides a fraction by long division and tells you what kind of decimal it produces. If the digits end, it's terminating; if a remainder repeats, the tool finds the repeating cycle and writes it in parentheses — the honest way to show 1/3.

Example

5/8 · and · 1/3…

↓

0.625 (terminating) · 0.(3) (repeating, period 1).

Use it for ratiosodds & probabilitydivision practiceprecision checks

Roman Numeral Converter

Converts in both directions across the full classical range, 1 to 3,999, using standard subtractive notation (IV, IX, XL, CM). Type roman numerals back and it parses them, checks whether the form is canonical, and shows the value broken into its symbols.

Example

2026 → roman · and · MMXXVI → number…

↓

MMXXVI = 1000 + 1000 + 10 + 10 + 5 + 1 = 2026.

Number to Words

Writes any integer up to the quadrillions in English words — with the short scale (one billion = 10⁹) — and handles negatives and decimal parts ("point four five"). It also produces the ordinal form: 26 becomes "twenty-sixth".

Example

1,234,567…

↓

one million two hundred thirty-four thousand five hundred sixty-seven.

Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
How does the Decimal to Fraction converter work?

It reads the decimal as text, so nothing is ever rounded by floating point. The digits after the point become the numerator over a power of ten (0.75 → 75/100), and the result is reduced with the greatest common divisor (75/100 → 3/4). Up to 12 decimal places are supported, and improper fractions are also shown as mixed numbers.

Which numbers can be written as roman numerals?

Positive integers from 1 to 3,999, using standard subtractive notation (IV = 4, IX = 9, XL = 40, CM = 900). The classical system has no symbol for zero. If you type a non-canonical form like IIII, the converter still parses it and shows you the canonical form IV.

What words does the Words to Number converter understand?

English short-scale number words: zero through nineteen, the tens (twenty to ninety), hundred, thousand, million, billion, trillion and quadrillion. Hyphens, commas and the word "and" are ignored, "minus" or "negative" sets the sign, and "point" starts a decimal part written as single digit words ("three point one four" → 3.14).

Is the data I enter saved anywhere?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. All conversions run inside your browser tab and disappear the moment you close or refresh the page. Your numbers and words are never transmitted, stored or logged.
