Common questions

Frequently asked questions about contacting us.

( 02 )
How quickly will I get a response?

We typically respond within 24-48 hours during weekdays. Since IrisTool is a one-person project, responses may take a bit longer on weekends or holidays, but we always read every message.

Can I request a new tool?

Absolutely! We love hearing tool ideas. Just send us an email with your suggestion and a brief description of what it should do. If it fits our privacy-first philosophy and runs entirely in the browser, we'll consider adding it.

Is my message stored anywhere?

Your message is sent directly to our email inbox via your email client. We don't have a database or backend — there's nothing to store your message in. We only keep the email to respond to you, and we delete it once the conversation is complete.

Do you offer paid support or custom tools?

All 278 tools on IrisTool are free and always will be. We don't offer paid support or custom tool development at this time, but we're open to partnership opportunities that align with our mission.

Can I report a bug anonymously?

Yes! You can send bug reports without including your name or email if you prefer. Just describe the issue, which tool it affects, and what browser/device you're using. We'll investigate and fix it.

Do you have social media accounts?

Not at the moment. We believe in keeping things simple and focused. Email is the best way to reach us, and we read every message personally.
