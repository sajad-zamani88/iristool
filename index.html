IrisTool has no database and no backend — literally. Every tool is plain JavaScript running inside your browser tab. There is nothing to hack, nothing to leak and nothing to delete, because we never receive your data in the first place. Close the tab and it's gone.

Is IrisTool really free?

Yes. All 278 tools are free with no signup, no usage limits and no premium tier. There are no servers to pay for per request, so there is nothing to charge you for.

Is my data safe here?

Completely. The site has no backend and no database — every tool runs entirely in your browser. Text you paste and images you open are never transmitted, logged or stored anywhere.

Do I need to create an account?

No account, no email, no tracking cookies. Open any tool and use it immediately — that's the whole idea.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, most tools keep working offline because all the logic runs locally in your browser.
