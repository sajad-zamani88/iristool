Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
What does the compressor actually do?

It re-encodes your image on a canvas at the quality you choose. In Auto mode it encodes both JPG and WebP and keeps whichever is smaller. The preview and the size comparison update instantly, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Why can the PNG output be bigger than my source?

PNG is lossless — it stores every pixel exactly, which for photos is usually larger than lossy formats. For photos, use Auto, JPG or WebP. PNG makes sense only for graphics with flat colors or when you must keep every pixel identical.

How does the target file size mode work?

The Quality Reducer runs a binary search on the JPG quality setting — up to eight encoding passes — and keeps the highest quality that still fits under your target in kilobytes. If even quality 5% is too big for the target, it gives you the smallest it can make.

Does my image ever leave my device?

No. Upload, compression and export all happen inside your browser tab using the canvas API. The page has no backend, so there is nowhere for the image to go — not even to a CDN.
