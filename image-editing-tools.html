Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
Does my image ever leave my device?

No. Upload, processing and export all happen inside your browser tab using the canvas API. The page has no backend, so there is nowhere for the image to go — not even to a CDN.

What resolution is processed?

For speed, the working copy is scaled down to at most 900 px on its longest side. The PNG you copy or download is the processed result at that working resolution, which is ideal for web, social and preview use.

Which formats can I upload, and what do I get out?

Anything your browser can decode — JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF — via click or drag-and-drop. Export is always lossless PNG. Animated GIFs are processed as their first frame.

What do the blur and sharpen filters actually do?

Blur is a two-pass separable box blur with an adjustable radius. Sharpen applies a 3×3 convolution kernel whose strength you control. Both run pixel-by-pixel on a canvas — no server filters, no CSS tricks.
