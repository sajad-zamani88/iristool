Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
How are fuel economy units related?

L/100 km and MPG are inversely related: lower L/100 km is better, while higher MPG is better. The exact conversions are MPG (US) = 235.215 ÷ (L/100 km) and MPG (imperial) = 282.481 ÷ (L/100 km). Kilometres per litre is simply distance divided by fuel used.

What does the Pace Calculator assume?

A constant pace on flat ground. Real races include hills, fatigue, aid stations and crowds, so treat the projections as targets rather than promises — most runners slow by a few percent over a full marathon.

Which race distances are used?

The official road distances: 5K = 5 km, 10K = 10 km, half marathon = 21.0975 km and marathon = 42.195 km.

Is the data I enter saved anywhere?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. All calculations run inside your browser tab and disappear the moment you close or refresh the page. Your numbers are never transmitted, stored or logged.
