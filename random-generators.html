Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
How random are the results?

All draws use JavaScript's Math.random(), which is uniform and perfectly fine for games, picks and casual use — but it is not cryptographically secure. Never use it for passwords, tokens, secrets or real-money stakes.

Are the team generator and wheel spinner fair?

Yes. The team generator uses a Fisher–Yates shuffle, so every ordering of your list is equally likely, and names are dealt round-robin for balanced team sizes. The wheel picks each option with equal probability, and every dice face, coin side, letter and date is uniformly distributed.

Can I reuse these tools offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, every generator keeps working offline, because all the randomness is produced locally in your browser — there is no server round-trip to fail.

Is the data I enter saved anywhere?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. Everything runs inside your browser tab and disappears the moment you close or refresh the page. Your name lists, quotes and wheel options are never transmitted, stored or logged.
