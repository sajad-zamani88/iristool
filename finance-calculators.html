Discount Calculator

VAT Calculator

Works both directions: add VAT to a net amount (net × (1 + rate)) or remove VAT from a gross total (gross ÷ (1 + rate)) to recover the net and the tax portion. A quantity field turns a unit price into full invoice totals.

Example

$500.00 net · VAT 20% · add…

↓

VAT $100.00 → gross $600.00 · removing from $600 returns the same split.

ROI Calculator

Measures any investment in the only way that matters: (final − initial) ÷ initial. On top of the total ROI it computes the annualized (geometric) return and the growth multiple, so a 3-year gain and a 10-year gain become honestly comparable.

ROI 35% · annualized ≈ 10.5% · 1.35× multiple.

Break-even Calculator

The one number every product needs before launch: fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost). It returns the units and revenue where costs and income meet, the contribution margin ratio, and how many units it takes to hit a target profit.

Example

↓

Break-even at 500 units ($25,000 revenue) · margin 40%.

( 02 )
Which formulas do these calculators use?

Loan and EMI payments use the amortization formula P·r ÷ (1 − (1+r)^−n), where r is the monthly rate and n the number of payments. Compound interest uses P(1+r/m)^(m·t) plus an annuity term for monthly contributions. Simple interest is P·r·t. ROI is (final − initial) ÷ initial, annualized geometrically. Break-even is fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost). VAT adds or removes (1 + rate) depending on direction.

Can I use these results for financial decisions?

The math is exact for the formulas shown, but the models are simplified. Real-world loans can include origination fees, different compounding conventions and rate changes; real returns include taxes and volatility. For significant decisions — mortgages, investments, taxes — confirm the numbers with a qualified financial professional.

Is the data I enter saved anywhere?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. All calculations run inside your browser tab and disappear the moment you close or refresh the page. Your amounts and rates are never transmitted, stored or logged.

What is the difference between the Loan and EMI calculators?

Both use the same amortization formula. The Loan Calculator takes the term in years and also shows the payoff date, while the EMI Calculator takes the term in months and breaks your first instalment down into its interest and principal parts, plus the daily cost of the loan.

Can I use these calculators offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, every calculator keeps working offline, because all the math runs locally in your browser — there is no server round-trip to fail.
