Remove Extra Spaces

runs in the bench above

Collapses every run of two or more spaces into one, trims spaces from the start and end of each line, and turns non-breaking spaces into ordinary ones. It's the single most common fix for text pasted from PDFs, where spacing always arrives damaged.

Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
What's the difference between Remove Blank Lines and Remove Empty Lines?

An empty line has zero characters — just a line break. A blank line may hide spaces or tabs while still looking empty on screen. Remove Empty Lines deletes only truly empty lines; Remove Blank Lines deletes both empty lines and whitespace-only lines. Turn both on for a full collapse.

Are duplicate lines and words matched case-sensitively?

Duplicate Lines is exact and case-sensitive — "Apple" and "apple" are different lines. Duplicate Words is case-insensitive — "The" and "the" count as the same word. Both filters keep the first occurrence and delete every later repeat, preserving the original order.

Why did my paragraph breaks disappear?

Because a line filter was enabled — collapsing the gaps between paragraphs is exactly what removing blank or empty lines does. Turn both line filters off to keep paragraph structure, and use Remove Extra Spaces alone for a lighter cleanup.

Is the text I paste saved anywhere?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. All cleanup runs inside your browser tab and disappears the moment you close or refresh the page. There is nothing to leak, because nothing is ever collected.

Can I use these tools offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, every filter on it keeps working offline, because all the logic runs locally in your browser.
