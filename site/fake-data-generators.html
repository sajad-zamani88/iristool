Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
Is any of this data real?

No. Every record is synthetic. US phone numbers use the fictional 555-01xx range reserved for testing, UK numbers use Ofcom's drama range, and card numbers are built from publicly documented test IINs with a valid Luhn checksum. None of them can authorize a real payment or identify a real person.

What is the Luhn checksum?

A simple mod-10 checksum used by card networks to catch typos: digits are doubled in alternating positions from the right, summed, and the final digit makes the total divisible by 10. A Luhn-valid test number passes form validation but still fails real authorization — which is exactly what you want in a test fixture.

What can I safely use these for?

Test fixtures, screenshots, mockups, demos, seed data and form validation. Never present generated data as a real person's, never feed real personal data into any generator — including this one — and never use generated card numbers against a live payment endpoint.

Is anything I generate saved anywhere?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. Everything is produced by JavaScript inside your browser tab and disappears the moment you close or refresh the page.
