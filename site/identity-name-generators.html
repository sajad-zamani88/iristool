Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
Are the generated names available?

The tools generate suggestions, not availability checks. Always verify domains at a registrar, search the social platform for existing handles, and run a trademark search before using a name commercially. Popular words are often already taken somewhere.

How does the generation work?

Each tool combines your keyword with curated word banks and naming patterns — adjectives, prefixes, suffixes, blends and style templates — then picks eight unique candidates using local randomness. Change any field or press Generate to roll a fresh set.

Can I use the generated names commercially?

The combinations are generic word pairings, so they are generally free to use, but you are responsible for clearing them: check company registries and trademark databases in your jurisdiction before launch, and secure the matching domain early.

Is the data I enter saved anywhere?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. All generation runs inside your browser tab and disappears the moment you close or refresh the page. Your keywords are never transmitted, stored or logged.
