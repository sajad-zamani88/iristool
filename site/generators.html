Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
Which generators does IrisTool offer?

39 generators across 7 groups: identity and name, random, developer, CSS, design, password and fake data. Each group has its own page listing every generator in it, with live previews and one-click regenerations.

Are the generated passwords safe?

Yes. Passwords are generated locally in your browser using crypto-grade randomness and are never transmitted, logged or stored anywhere. The strength checker runs on your device too, so the password you test is never sent away.

Can I use generated names and fake data commercially?

Yes. Everything you generate on this page is yours to use however you like — in projects, mockups, tests, even production. The generation runs on your device and nothing is saved or claimed.

Is anything I generate saved anywhere?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. Every generator runs inside your browser tab and the output disappears when you close or refresh the page. There is nothing to leak, because nothing is ever collected.

Can I use the generators offline?

Once the page has loaded, every generator keeps working offline, because all the logic runs locally in your browser.
