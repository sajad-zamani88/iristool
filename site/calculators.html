Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
Which calculators does IrisTool offer?

60 calculators across 8 groups: health, finance, date and time, education and statistics, math, unit converters, number converters and fitness. Every group has its own page listing all of its calculators end to end.

Are your formulas accurate?

Each calculator uses the standard, widely accepted formula for its domain — BMI, compound interest, GPA and so on — and the page states which formula it uses so you can verify it yourself.

Are my health and finance numbers saved anywhere?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. Every calculation happens inside your browser tab and your inputs disappear the moment you close or refresh the page. There is nothing to leak, because nothing is ever collected.

Are the calculators really free?

Yes. All 60 calculators are free with no signup, no usage limits and no premium tier. Because they run in your browser there are no per-use server costs, so there is nothing to charge you for.

Do the calculators work on mobile?

Yes. Every page is responsive and works in any modern browser on any device. No installation, no app to download.
