Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
Which unit systems are supported?

Metric and US customary. Volume uses US gallons, cups, tablespoons and teaspoons — the UK imperial gallon is about 20% larger and is not included. All factors use the modern exact definitions: 1 inch = 2.54 cm, 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg, 1 atmosphere = 101,325 Pa.

Why are temperature conversions not a simple multiplication?

Because the zero points differ. The converter pivots through Celsius: it first converts the input to °C (from °F: subtract 32, multiply by 5/9; from K: subtract 273.15) and then converts from °C to the target scale. A difference of 1 °C always equals 1.8 °F or 1 K.

How does the Pace Converter work?

Enter a distance (km, miles or metres) and the time it took. It returns pace per kilometre and per mile, speed in km/h and mph, and projected 5K, 10K and marathon finish times, assuming the same pace is held for the whole race.

Is the data I enter saved anywhere?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. All conversions run inside your browser tab and disappear the moment you close or refresh the page. Your values are never transmitted, stored or logged.
