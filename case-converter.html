Uppercase Converter

Lowercase Converter

runs in the converter above

The Lowercase Converter folds every capital letter down to small case. It's the standard fix for text pasted from PDFs, slides or ALL-CAPS emails, and the right first step before building URL slugs, comparing strings or normalising data for a spreadsheet.

Title Case Converter

runs in the converter above

The Title Case Converter capitalises the first letter of every word and lowercases the rest — the standard treatment for headlines, book titles and navigation labels. Unlike Capitalize Words, it also fixes stray capitals hiding in the middle of words.

Example

"the QUICK brown fox jumps"

↓

"The Quick Brown Fox Jumps" — "QUICK" is tamed, not just capped.

Use it for headlines & titlesnav labelsYouTube titlesfile names

Sentence Case Converter

runs in the converter above

The Sentence Case Converter lowercases everything, then capitalises only the first letter of each sentence — the style preferred by most modern style guides for body copy. It finds sentence boundaries by their terminal punctuation (. ! ? …) and the start of new lines.

Example

"hello WORLD. how ARE you? fine!"

↓

"Hello world. How are you? Fine!" — one capital per sentence, nothing more.

Use it for body copystyle-guide cleanupessays & reportsUI text

Toggle Case

runs in the converter above

Toggle Case flips the case of every single letter: uppercase becomes lowercase and lowercase becomes uppercase, character by character. Numbers, spaces and punctuation pass through untouched. It's the classic sWaP-cAsE operation programmers know as swapcase.

Example

"Hello World 2026"

↓

"hELLO wORLD 2026" — each letter swapped, digits kept.

Use it for case repairobfuscationstylistic effectstesting & QA

Capitalize Words

runs in the converter above

Capitalize Words uppercases only the first letter of each word and leaves every other character exactly as you wrote it. Use it when the original casing carries meaning — product names, part numbers, codes — and a full Title Case conversion would destroy information.

Example

"hello WORLD product X9"

↓

"Hello WORLD Product X9" — "WORLD" and "X9" keep their casing.

Use it for product namespart numbersnames & listsgentle capitalisation

07 / 07

Invert Case

runs in the converter above

The Invert Case converter rewrites your text in alternating case, starting with a lowercase letter: hElLo wOrLd. Only letters count toward the alternation — spaces, digits and punctuation are skipped without breaking the rhythm. A favourite for memes, usernames and deliberately chaotic styling.

Example

"Hello World"

↓

"hElLo wOrLd" — alternating letter by letter, spaces skipped.

Use it for memes & jokesusernamesDiscord / Steam namesvisual noise

Conversion rules · title = first letter up, rest down · capitalize = first letter up, rest untouched · sentence = capital after . ! ? … and new lines · toggle = swap every letter · invert = alternate, starting lowercase · non-letters never change

Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
What's the difference between Title Case and Capitalize Words?

Title Case lowercases the rest of every word as it capitalizes the first letter — "the QUICK fox" becomes "The Quick Fox". Capitalize Words only touches the first letter of each word and leaves everything else exactly as you typed it — "the QUICK fox" becomes "The QUICK Fox". Use Title Case for headlines; use Capitalize Words when the original casing carries meaning.

What exactly do Toggle Case and Invert Case do?

Toggle Case swaps the case of every letter, one by one: "Hello" becomes "hELLO". Invert Case rewrites the text in alternating case starting with a lowercase letter: "Hello World" becomes "hElLo wOrLd". In both tools, spaces, digits and punctuation are never altered — and in Invert Case they don't count toward the alternation.

Does Sentence case know my proper nouns?

No — no case converter can tell that "paris" is a name. It lowercases everything, then capitalizes the first letter after each sentence boundary (. ! ? … and new lines). Run it first, then re-capitalize names, brands and acronyms by hand.

Is the text I paste saved anywhere?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. All conversion runs inside your browser tab and disappears the moment you close or refresh the page. There is nothing to leak, because nothing is ever collected.

Can I use these tools offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, every tool on it keeps working offline, because all the logic runs locally in your browser.
