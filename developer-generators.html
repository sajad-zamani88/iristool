Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

( 02 )
Are the generated UUIDs unique?

UUID v4 values are built from crypto.getRandomValues(), so each one carries 122 bits of randomness. You would need to generate on the order of 10¹⁸ of them before a collision becomes likely, which is why v4 is the default for databases, sessions and file identifiers.

What is the difference between UUID and GUID here?

They are the same 128-bit value. The GUID tool formats it the way Microsoft-style stacks traditionally print it: uppercase and wrapped in braces — {XXXXXXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXXXXXXXXX} — with the braces optional.

Which barcode format does the Barcode Generator use?

Code 39, one of the most widely supported 1-D symbologies. It accepts A–Z, 0–9, space and the symbols - . $ / + %, and wraps your text in the standard * start/stop markers automatically. Unsupported characters are stripped before encoding.

Is the data I enter saved anywhere?

No. IrisTool has no backend and no database. QR codes, barcodes, IDs and dummy data are all produced locally in your browser tab and disappear the moment you close or refresh the page. Your payloads are never transmitted, stored or logged.
